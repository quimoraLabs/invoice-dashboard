## 2. Market Positioning Statement
"For freelancers and small agency operators who find traditional accounting platforms bloated and slow, **Invoice Dashboard** is a lightweight, zero-cost invoicing tool that turns billing data into printable PDFs and financial analytics in under 60 seconds."
